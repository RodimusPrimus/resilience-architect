Disaster Recovery as a Business Tradeoff

Every organization faces a fundamental tradeoff: how much are you willing to invest in infrastructure redundancy versus how much downtime and data loss can your business tolerate?

This tool maps your workloads to AWS Disaster Recovery strategies based on your Recovery Time Objective (RTO) and Recovery Point Objective (RPO) targets, aligned with business criticality. The result is a clear, executive-ready view of your DR posture.
